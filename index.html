About

I am a Materials and Chemical Engineering researcher pursuing an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's across the University of Miskolc (Hungary) and the University of Liège (Belgium), achieving a GPA of 4.8/5 in my first year.

My research focuses on aluminium alloy design, control of Fe-rich intermetallic phases, phase-field modelling, and sustainable metallurgy — particularly the recycling of scrap Al–Si alloys. I collaborate with CNRS–Institut Jean Lamour (France) and IIT Hyderabad.

I have authored or co-authored 13+ peer-reviewed publications in journals including JOM, Scientific Reports, Tribology Transactions, and Transactions of the Indian Institute of Metals.

I am also a Gandhi Fellow and former NITI Fellow at the Piramal Foundation — an 11-month leadership programme building empathy-driven problem solvers.
